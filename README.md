## 🚀 Live Demo & Calculator Suite

Access the live calculators directory:  
👉 **[https://mymoneyfuture.com/calculators/](https://mymoneyfuture.com/calculators/)**

### Included Calculators:
1. **[Loan EMI & Amortization Calculator](https://mymoneyfuture.com/calculators/loan-calculator/)**  
   Calculate monthly loan installments, total interest payable, and interactive principal-to-interest breakdowns.
2. **[Compound Interest & SIP Calculator](https://mymoneyfuture.com/calculators/compound-interest-calculator/)**  
   Project wealth accumulation with periodic SIP contributions and customizable compounding frequencies.
3. **[Retirement & FIRE Number Planner](https://mymoneyfuture.com/calculators/retirement-calculator/)**  
   Calculate your exact Financial Independence number based on the Trinity Study 4% Safe Withdrawal Rate rule.
4. **[Debt Snowball & Avalanche Payoff Calculator](https://mymoneyfuture.com/calculators/debt-payoff-calculator/)**  
   Compare mathematical interest savings (Avalanche) vs. psychological momentum (Snowball) strategies.
5. **[Emergency Fund Calculator](https://mymoneyfuture.com/calculators/emergency-fund-calculator/)**  
   Determine personal 3-to-6 month cash reserves based on housing, utilities, food, and recurring essentials.
6. **[50/30/20 Monthly Budget Calculator](https://mymoneyfuture.com/calculators/budget-calculator/)**  
   Allocate net take-home salary into Needs (50%), Wants (30%), and Savings/Debt Repayment (20%).
7. **[Mortgage & Home Loan Calculator](https://mymoneyfuture.com/calculators/mortgage-calculator/)**  
   Simulate 15-year and 30-year fixed home loans with P&I monthly breakdown and visual amortization charts.
